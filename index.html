Overview

Mahaavir Labdhi

A Serene Sea-Facing Residential Landmark Rising Across 6.5 Acres in Uran, Navi Mumbai.
Mahaavir Labdhi is primely positioned in Uran’s most prestigious growth corridor, offering uninterrupted panoramic views of the Arabian Sea. Designed for those who seek refined living amidst nature's grandeur, this majestic gated enclave features high-rise premium towers, the largest podium garden in the region, and world-class club life leisures. From meticulously planned 2 & 3 BHK luxury residences to seamless connectivity via the Atal Setu, Mahaavir Labdhi brings you a lifestyle where elite architecture meets absolute serenity.
